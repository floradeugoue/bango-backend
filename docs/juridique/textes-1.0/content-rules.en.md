# Content rules

### 1. The spirit of these rules

BANGO is for finding a home and keeping a neighbourhood alive. These rules exist so that both stay possible. They apply to everything: posts, reels, stories, comments, listings, messages, handles and profile photos.

### 2. Listings: truth first

A listing must match a **real** property, **available**, and one you have the right to offer.

Prohibited:

• the bait listing — an attractive property that does not exist, used to pull contacts towards another;
• photos that are not of the property, or that show an earlier state without saying so;
• a price shown that differs from the price actually asked, or undisclosed fees discovered at the viewing;
• keeping an already-let property online. Mark it taken; the app does the rest and warns the people who had a viewing booked.

### 3. Money: what is never asked for

Never ask for, and never pay, money before a viewing. Prohibited: "file fees" demanded before seeing the property, requests to transfer to a personal account, and any pressure to pay quickly to "reserve".

Report immediately any message asking you for money outside the means provided by the app.

### 4. Discrimination

Refusing a tenant because of their origin, nationality, ethnicity, religion, sex, family situation, health or a disability is prohibited, and writing it in a listing equally so.

Objective criteria about solvency and ability to occupy the property remain legitimate.

### 5. Harassment and hate

Prohibited: insults, threats, content inciting hatred or violence against a person or a group, publishing private information without agreement, and repeatedly contacting someone who has asked you to stop.

Blocking is not reporting. If behaviour is abusive, report it: otherwise moderation will not see it.

### 6. Sexual, violent and shocking content

Sexual nudity and pornographic content are prohibited. So are gratuitous violence, content showing serious injuries, and content glorifying suicide or eating disorders.

### 7. Minors

Any content sexualising minors is prohibited, reported to the competent authorities, and leads to immediate and permanent closure of the account. There is no appeal.

### 8. False identities

Prohibited: passing yourself off as another person, an agency, an administration or BANGO; using someone else's photo as your profile picture; displaying a badge or verification ring obtained with a forged document.

### 9. Spam and manipulation

Prohibited: repetitive posting, buying or trading followers, likes or comments, using multiple accounts to inflate an audience, and misleading links.

### 10. Illegal activity

Prohibited: any content offering, organising or facilitating illegal activity — sale of regulated goods, counterfeiting, unlicensed gambling, unauthorised financial services.

### 11. Other people’s property

Do not publish photos, videos, music or texts you have no rights to. A rights holder can report content to us; we remove it and tell the person who published it, who can challenge.

### 12. Promotion

Promoted content must respect all the rules above, and two more: it does not promise a home that does not exist, and it does not advertise a price it will not honour.

### 13. What happens when a rule is broken

The response is proportionate and progressive: a warning, then a temporary posting suspension, then closure of the account. The most serious breaches — content involving minors, established fraud, impersonation — lead to immediate closure.

You are always told, with the reason and the content involved, and you may challenge once. A warning is removed from your file after six months without a further incident.

### 14. Reporting

Every piece of content, every profile and every conversation carries a report option. Report as often as needed: a report that leads to no action never counts against you.

If you are in immediate danger, contact your country's emergency services first. The app is not an emergency service.
