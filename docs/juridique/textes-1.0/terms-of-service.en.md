### 6. Subscriptions, prices and renewal

Badges are taken monthly or yearly. The price shown before payment is the price due, in your country's currency, excluding any fees charged by your payment operator.

**Renewal.** The subscription renews automatically at the due date unless you turn off auto-renewal from the My subscriptions screen. We tell you before each due date.

**Changing plan.** Moving from monthly to yearly or the other way triggers a pro rata calculation: the unused part of the current period is deducted from the amount due. The breakdown is shown before payment.

**Failed charge.** If a charge fails, the badge stays active during a **seven-day grace period**, during which we retry the payment. After that, with nothing settled, the badge expires.

**Cancellation.** You may cancel at any time. The badge stays active until the end of the period already paid for. **No refund is due for a period started**, except where the law of your country says otherwise.

### 7. What happens when a badge expires

Nothing is deleted. That is a commitment on our part, and the platform keeps it:

• your listings are **unpublished**, not erased. They leave search and the property feed, and come back exactly as they were — photos, texts, statistics, conversations — as soon as you reactivate;
• the access you had granted to agents goes **dormant**. Those agents are notified, their ongoing conversations become read-only and come back to you;
• any running promotion campaign stops, and the unspent budget comes back as BANGO credit;
• your account, posts, reels, followers, messaging and payment history are untouched.

The budget is committed when payment is approved and spent as delivery runs. If the campaign stops before the end — by your choice, by a moderation decision, or because the content was deleted — the unspent part comes back as **BANGO credit**.

We may refuse or interrupt a promotion whose content breaches the Content Rules, without that giving rise to anything beyond the return of the unspent budget.

### 9. BANGO credit

BANGO credit is an internal unit of account, usable to pay for a badge, a promotion or viewing fees.

• It **cannot be bought**. It comes only from an unspent campaign budget, a settled double charge, or a goodwill gesture on our part.
• It **cannot be withdrawn** in Mobile Money, converted to cash, or transferred to another account.
• It does not expire while the account exists. **It disappears with the account**: use it before deleting.

### 10. Payments and Mobile Money

Payments go through Mobile Money operators and, in some countries, bank cards. We are not the payment institution: the transaction is carried out by the operator, under its own terms.

**Your PIN never passes through the app.** You enter it on your phone, in your operator's interface. No BANGO screen will ever ask for it; if that happens, it is an attempted fraud and we ask you to report it.

If you are charged twice, report it from the app with the transaction reference and your operator's SMS. After checking with them, the extra amount is credited back as BANGO credit within 72 hours.

If the network drops during an approval, **do not retry the payment**: you would risk a double charge. The app queries the operator and tells you as soon as the status is known.

A viewing fee pays for the organisation and travel of the person who receives you. **It is not a reservation of the property, a deposit, or a guarantee of tenancy.**

Cancellation: refunds follow the terms shown at booking time. If the landlord or agent cancels, or if the property turns out to be unavailable, the fee is refunded in full.

### 12. Delegating to an agent

A landlord may authorise an agent to act on their properties. Authorisation is given **capability by capability**: run viewings, answer messages, publish, edit a listing.

Two things are never delegated: **changing a price** and **taking payment**.

Every action by an agent is logged under their name. The landlord can withdraw access at any time; the agent is told, their conversations become read-only and return to the landlord. A revoked agent is never erased: their history stays readable, because it may serve as proof of a past exchange.
