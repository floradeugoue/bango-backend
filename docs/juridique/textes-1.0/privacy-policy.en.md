# Privacy Policy

### 1. This document on one page

Here is the essence; the detail follows:

• **We do not sell your data.** Ever, to anyone.
• We collect what is needed to run the app, and we say what for each time.
• Your precise location is used only if you allow it, and only at the moment you use it.
• Your private conversations are neither read nor analysed for advertising. They are consulted only where something is reported, and only by the people responsible for moderation.
• You can view, correct, export and delete your data from the app at any time.
• Your identity documents are stored separately, with restricted access, and deleted as soon as they are no longer needed.

### 2. Who is responsible

CCEDA Finance is the controller of your personal data within BANGO. For any question about this policy or to exercise your rights: confidentialite@bangoapp.com.

BANGO operates in several countries. Depending on where you live, your supervisory authority may be the Cameroonian personal data protection authority (Cameroon), the Commission de protection des données personnelles (Senegal), the Autorité de régulation des télécommunications (Côte d'Ivoire), the Data Protection Commission (Ghana), the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, or the competent authority in your country. Up-to-date contact details are in the Help Centre.

### 3. Data you give us

**At sign-up**: email address or identifier from the authentication provider, password, phone number, date of birth, country.

**While using the app**: handle, display name, profile photo, bio, interests, neighbourhood, language.

**When you publish**: texts, photos, videos, stories, comments, property listings and their characteristics.

**When you talk**: private messages, attachments, voice notes.

**When you pay**: amount, currency, operator, masked wallet number, transaction reference and status. **We never receive your PIN or a full card number.**

**When you apply for a badge**: identity document (front and back), proof of your right to a property (title deed, lease or management mandate) and a liveness selfie, meaning three photos of your face taken live.

**Biometric data.** With your express consent, given on the selfie screen, a specialised identity verification provider compares your face with the photo on your identity document and checks that a real person is present. This comparison is used only to verify your identity. Our compliance team then reviews the result. If you refuse the automatic comparison, write to support: a person will review your file.

**Your contacts**, if you allow it: your phone computes a fingerprint of each number in your address book and sends it to us, to show you who is already on BANGO. Your contacts' numbers and names never leave your phone, and the fingerprints are used only for this comparison.

### 4. Data we observe

**Usage**: screens opened, content seen, searches made, filters set, properties saved. Used to show what interests you and to measure what works.

**Device**: model, operating system, app version, language, time zone, technical install identifier. Used for diagnostics and security.

**Connection**: IP address, sign-in dates and times, devices used. Used to detect unusual access and fraud attempts.

**Location**: only if you allow it, and only to centre a map, sort properties by distance, pre-fill a city or check that you live in the neighbourhood you chose (“Neighbour” badge). Your precise location is never published. You can withdraw that permission in your phone settings; the app keeps working and asks for your city instead.

### 5. What we do not collect

• No health data, political opinions, religious beliefs, trade-union membership or sexual orientation. If you voluntarily publish such things in content, they follow that content's regime.
• No copy of your address book without your explicit permission, and never its contents: when you look for contacts already on BANGO, the comparison is done by fingerprint, and numbers matching nobody are not kept.
• No audio or video recording beyond what you deliberately produce.
• No cross-app advertising tracking.

### 6. Why we process this data

Every processing has a purpose and a legal basis:

• **Running the service** — account, publishing, messaging, search, viewings. *Performance of the contract.*
• **Charging and invoicing** — subscriptions, viewing fees, promotions. *Performance of the contract and accounting obligation.*
• **Verifying identity** — badges, KYC. *Performance of the contract and legal anti-fraud obligation.*
• **Securing** — detecting unusual access, rate limiting, protecting against impersonation. *Legitimate interest.*
• **Moderating** — handling reports, applying the Content Rules. *Legitimate interest and legal obligation.*
• **Improving** — aggregate statistics, usage measurement. *Legitimate interest.*
• **Telling you things** — service notifications. *Performance of the contract.* Promotional messages rest on your **consent** and stop in one tap.

### 7. Who sees what

**Public**: your handle, photo, bio, posts, reels, published listings and badges.

**Visible to the people you talk to only**: your messages, and your phone number if you give it out yourself in a conversation. Two exceptions: when a conversation or a message is reported to us, an authorised moderator may read the reported part to decide; and we may hand a conversation to an authority on a lawful request. Each look by our team is recorded in a log under their name.

**Visible to a landlord**: when you request a viewing, your display name, your message and the slot requested.

**Visible to an agent**: only the properties a landlord authorised them on, and the conversations attached to them. Each of their actions is logged under their name.

**Never public**: your email address, date of birth, identity documents, payment methods, transaction history, location.

### 8. Who we share with

**We sell no data.** We share only:

• with **payment operators**, what the transaction requires: amount, reference, wallet number;
• with our **technical processors**, bound by contract, acting on instruction, with no right of their own to use the data: server hosting (Contabo, Germany), media delivery and network protection (Cloudflare, United States), email delivery (Resend, United States), SMS delivery (Zomloa, Cameroon), notifications (Expo and Google Firebase, United States), identity verification and biometrics (specialised provider, depending on your country), automatic translation of content (Google or DeepL), animated image search (GIPHY, United States) and payment (Monetbil and the Mobile Money operators of your country);
• with **authorities**, on a lawful request, and only within what is asked. We tell you unless the law forbids it;
• in the event of a **sale** of the business, with the buyer, who stays bound by this policy until you accept theirs.

### 9. Where your data is hosted

Our servers are in the European Union (Germany). Several processors, listed in the previous article, handle data in the United States. Your data is therefore processed outside your country of residence.

Where that happens, the transfer is framed: standard contractual clauses, or any other mechanism recognised by the applicable law. You can ask us for the list of processors and countries involved.

### 10. How long we keep things

• **Active account**: while the account exists.
• **After deletion**: thirty days to change your mind, then erasure, subject to the reservations below.
• **Accounting records and payment evidence**: ten years, as accounting law requires.
• **Identity documents and KYC**: for the duration of the check, then five years after the relationship ends, on anti-fraud grounds.
• **Connection logs**: twelve months.
• **Deleted content**: removed from display immediately, from backups within thirty days.
• **Moderation decisions**: two years, to handle an appeal and apply progressive sanctions.
• **Messages**: while the conversation exists for at least one of the two people.

### 11. Your rights

At any time you may:

• **access** your data and obtain a copy;
• **correct** it if it is inaccurate;
• **erase** it, subject to our retention obligations;
• **restrict** a processing while you contest it;
• **object** to a processing based on our legitimate interest;
• **retrieve** your data in a machine-readable format, and ask us to send it to another service;
• **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting what was done before.

Most of these rights are exercised directly in the app: Settings → My data. The others by writing to confidentialite@bangoapp.com. **We answer within thirty days.** If we refuse, we say why, and you can refer the matter to your supervisory authority.

### 12. Automated decisions

We use automated processing to rank feed content, estimate the reach of a promotion, compare your face with your identity document during a verification, and spot suspicious content.

**The automatic text filter.** Posts, captions and comments go through a filter that spots scams, requests to bypass the platform, spam and offensive language. Depending on the alerts received over ninety days, it can automatically warn you (first time), remove the content concerned (second time), then restrict your account for seven days (third time). You are told each time, with the reason, and **you can challenge it** from the app: a person then reviews the decision.

Refusing a badge and permanently suspending an account are always decided by a person.

### 13. Security

We protect your data with proportionate technical and organisational measures:

• **Passwords**: never stored in the clear. We keep a salted cryptographic digest, from which the password cannot be recovered.
• **Transport**: encrypted end to end between the app and our servers.
• **Session**: kept in your phone's secure system store.
• **Lock**: you can require your fingerprint or face when the app opens.
• **Internal access**: restricted to the people who need it, logged, reviewed regularly.
• **Identity documents**: stored separately, encrypted, with named access.

No system is infallible. In the event of a breach likely to create a risk to your rights, we notify the competent authority and, where the risk is high, we tell you directly.

### 14. Working offline

The app keeps a copy of recently viewed content on your phone so it stays usable without a network. That copy is local, is not shared, and disappears when you sign out or delete the app.

### 15. Notifications and messages

**Service notifications** — a reply to a message, a viewing confirmation, a subscription due date, a moderation decision — are part of the service and are tuned in Settings → Notifications.

**Promotional messages** go out only with your agreement and stop in one tap, without affecting anything else.

### 16. Minors

The service is not intended for people under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you know of an account belonging to a minor, report it: we close it and delete the associated data.

### 17. People visible in your content

If a photo or video you publish shows a recognisable person, you must have their agreement. Anyone may ask us to remove content in which they appear, even without a BANGO account, by writing to confidentialite@bangoapp.com.

### 18. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy. Any substantial change is notified in the app thirty days before it takes effect, with a summary of what changed. The version in force and its date are always shown at the top of this screen.
